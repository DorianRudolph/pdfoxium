The user converted the DjVu with
[djvu2pdf2smol](https://github.com/dorianrudolph/djvu2pdf2smol), reencoding its
JB2 mask as JBIG2 and retaining the OCR text. The resulting PDF was supplied on
October 21, 2026 and is kept unchanged: one letter-size page, 16,845 bytes,
producer `djvu2pdf 0.1.0`, with a 3400 x 4400 JBIG2 stencil. This small converted
fixture is checked in specifically to exercise that encoding, rather than
requiring a DjVu converter at test time.
